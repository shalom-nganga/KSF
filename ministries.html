Our ministries

Find your place in the family.

Faith beyond four walls

Church Without Walls

Worship and the message of hope, shared wherever people are.+

When the pandemic closed church buildings, KSF carried worship and prayer into the digital world. Through television, radio and social media, Church Without Walls connects people across distances with the Gospel, encouragement and a community of faith.

Its vision is simple: a church without geographical boundaries, where people can receive spiritual nourishment and join in worship wherever they are.

Faith for the next generation

The Youth Ministry

A welcoming community for young people to grow in faith and purpose.+

The Youth Ministry supports young people as they grow spiritually, discover their gifts and navigate the move into adulthood. It offers an encouraging place to explore faith, ask questions and build friendships.

Young members are encouraged to carry their faith into everyday life, not only into Sunday services.

A strong start in faith

Children’s Ministry

Age-appropriate, joyful ways for children to learn about Jesus.+

Children are welcomed into a safe, caring environment where faith can take root. From infants through preteens, teaching is shaped for each age with creative activities such as storytelling, arts and crafts.

Alongside learning about Jesus, children are encouraged to practice kindness, love and compassion, make lasting friendships and take part in activities throughout the year.

The message in every home

Media Ministry

Faith, useful conversations and Gospel music on air and online.+

Launched with the acquisition of radio and television frequencies in 2015, MBCI TV and Radio bring KSF content to audiences near and far. MBCI TV broadcasts across Kenya, with programmes for families, children and young people, as well as talk shows, features and music.

MBCI Radio offers round-the-clock programming on sermons, family and community issues, business, health and agriculture, alongside Gospel music. Broadcasts are also available online so listeners can tune in from beyond Kenya.

Growing together

The Women’s Ministry

Faith, friendship and practical encouragement for women.+

Led by Rev. Naomi Kimani, the Women’s Ministry encourages women to grow spiritually and find support in every season of life. Bible study, prayer and fellowship create space to connect with God and with one another.

Its welcoming community nurtures resilience, friendship and service, with relationships that continue beyond church gatherings.

Faith in the public square

Billboard for Jesus

Creative ways to share the hope and love of Jesus in everyday places.+

Billboard for Jesus (B4J) shares the message of Jesus beyond church walls. The ministry uses billboards, banners, social media videos and other multimedia to make messages of faith, hope and love visible in public life.

B4J welcomes people who want to contribute their time, resources or creative talents to this work.

Compassion in action

Mercy Ministry

Practical help and spiritual encouragement for people in need.+

The Mercy Ministry puts compassion into action through food, clothing and shelter support. Its outreach has included helping families displaced by the 2007 post-election violence, organizing food drives during the COVID-19 pandemic, and delivering supplies to hunger-stricken areas.

The team also visits prisons, offering practical assistance, spiritual guidance and a message of hope and redemption.

Prayer and seeking God

Heaven’s Gate

A place set apart for prayer and time in God’s presence.+

Heaven’s Gate is a dedicated place for prayer, worship and seeking God. It is a spiritual home for people to pause, pray and draw closer to God together.

The World Revival Centre

W.R.C

A ministry focused on revival and renewed faith.+

The World Revival Centre (W.R.C) is part of the KSF ministry family, with a heart for spiritual renewal and revival. It invites people to grow in faith and seek God together.

Music ministry

Kingdom Band

Musicians using their gifts to support worship and serve the church.+

Kingdom Band brings musicians together to serve through music. With instruments and a shared love for worship, the band helps lead the congregation in praise and creates space for people to focus on God.

It is a place to grow in musical skill, work together as a team and use creative gifts in service to the KSF family.

Worship ministry

KSF Voices

A vocal ministry helping the congregation worship together.+

KSF Voices is a community of singers who use their voices to lead worship and lift up the name of Jesus. Through song, they help create a welcoming atmosphere for the congregation to praise, reflect and draw near to God.

More than a choir, KSF Voices is a team growing together in faith, harmony and service.
