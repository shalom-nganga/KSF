Welcome to Kingdom Seekers Fellowship

A church thatloves God and people.

Kingdom Seekers Fellowship was founded by Apostle John Kimani William and Rev. Naomi Kimani, who have continued to minister the word of God to an ever-growing congregation. The ministry is headquartered in Nakuru Town, with over 20 branches spreading across the country.

In order to win more souls for the Kingdom of God, Kingdom Seekers Fellowship, through its Overseer Apostle John Kimani William, launched a ministry in 2008 called Missions to the Body of Christ International (MBCI). MBCI has traversed every major city and town in Kenya with life-transforming Prayer and Breakthrough Conferences.
